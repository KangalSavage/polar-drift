Common questions

How do I play?
Tap anywhere to flip your ball's charge between red and blue. Matching an oncoming wall's color repels you safely past it; the opposite color pulls you in, which ends your run. Collect gems for combo score and rare shield pickups to survive one wrong-charge mistake.

How do I unlock ball/trail themes?
Themes are unlocked by spending coins you earn at the end of every run, based on your score.

How do I turn off sound?
Tap the speaker icon in the top-left corner of the main menu.
