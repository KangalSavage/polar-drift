Polar Drift - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Polar Drift ("the app") is a mobile game published by Sparkform. This page explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Data the app stores only on your device

The app saves the following locally on your device, using standard iOS app storage. This data is never sent to any server we operate:

  • Your best score
  • Your coin balance and which cosmetic ball/trail themes you've unlocked or selected
  • Whether you've seen the first-time tutorial prompt
  • Your sound/haptics on-off preference

We do not require or offer account creation, and we do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal profile information.

Advertising

Polar Drift shows ads (rewarded video and interstitial ads) served through Google AdMob to keep the app free to play. To do this, AdMob and its partners may collect and process data such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), general device information, and ad interaction data, in order to serve and measure ads. On iOS, you will be asked for permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any identifier is used for ad tracking; if you decline, you'll still see ads, just less personalized ones.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us directly. You can review how Google handles this data in Google's own Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and see AdMob's data disclosure at https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

Children's privacy

Polar Drift is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, if we add analytics or new features that affect data collection), we will update this page.
